Handmade apps that save you clicks

Made in Kuwait by ilukezippo, also known as BoYaoqub

Free, open-source apps for Windows, Steam Deck and Android. Each one does a single job well: convert files, fix contacts, fetch subtitles, update apps, repair Windows, download videos on your phone, turn a Galaxy Z Fold into a laptop and make games easier to play. In English and Arabic.

The apps

Hi, I'm BoYaoqub

I'm a self-taught developer from Kuwait, known on GitHub as ilukezippo. Every app on this page started as a small annoyance in my own day: a HEIC photo that wouldn't open, a contacts file Windows couldn't read, a game that wanted me to mash one button for an hour.

Instead of putting up with it, I build the tool, polish it until I'd happily use it every day, and share it here for free. Ideas and bug reports are always welcome on GitHub.
